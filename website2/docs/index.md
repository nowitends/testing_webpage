# Chile Field Guide

Chile stretches along South America's Pacific edge, from the Atacama Desert in
the north to the fjords, forests, and glaciers of Patagonia. This field guide
brings together the project's research notes on the country's landscapes,
people, astronomy, and culture.

## Explore the guide

- [Country overview](country-overview.md) — essential facts and geographic
  regions.
- [Population](population.md) — 2024 census indicators and population by
  region.
- [Nature and places](nature-and-places.md) — notable places and natural
  phenomena.
- [Astronomy](astronomy.md) — observatories and questions about Chile's skies.
- [History and culture](history-culture.md) — selected milestones, food, and
  cultural topics.
- [Visualization ideas](visualization-ideas.md) — potential features and
  datasets for future work.

## About the data

Population figures are labeled with their census reference. Coordinates for
places are approximate markers, not precise boundaries or navigation
waypoints. Observatory altitudes are rounded; check each facility's official
website for current status and schedules.

Use the source links on each page to verify information before reusing it.
