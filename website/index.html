04 · Forces that shape the land

A landscape in motion.

Climate, tectonic forces and rapidly changing weather help explain the contrasts along Chile's length.

01 / NORTH

The Atacama Desert

Among Earth's driest non-polar regions. Atmospheric circulation, the Andes rain shadow and the cold Humboldt Current contribute to its aridity. Unusual rainfall can trigger spectacular flowering.

02 / ANDES

Mountains & volcanoes

The Nazca Plate subducts beneath the South American Plate, helping drive Andean mountain building and volcanism. The plate boundary is also associated with major earthquakes.

03 / SOUTH

Patagonia

Fjords, glaciers, temperate forests and the Southern Patagonian Ice Field shape the south. Weather can change rapidly, and strong winds are characteristic of many exposed places.

06 · History & everyday life

Stories carried through time.

A short selection of historical milestones alongside dishes and cultural topics from across the country.

  1. Before European arrival

    Diverse Indigenous societies, including Mapuche, Aymara, Rapa Nui and others.

  2. 1541

    Santiago founded by Pedro de Valdivia.

  3. 1810–1818

    Independence process begins; independence formally proclaimed in 1818.

  4. 1879–1884

    War of the Pacific.

  5. 1960

    Valdivia earthquake, magnitude 9.5 (moment magnitude estimate).

  6. 1973

    Military coup; beginning of Augusto Pinochet's dictatorship.

  7. 1988

    Plebiscite rejects continuation of Pinochet's rule.

  8. 1990

    Return to civilian democratic government.

  9. 2010

    Major Maule earthquake and tsunami.
