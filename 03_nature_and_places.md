## Natural phenomena worth explaining

### Atacama Desert

The Atacama is among the driest non-polar regions on Earth. Atmospheric circulation, the Andes rain shadow and the cold Humboldt Current contribute to its aridity. Some locations experience spectacular flowering after unusual rainfall events.

### The Andes and volcanoes

The Nazca Plate subducts beneath the South American Plate, helping drive Andean mountain building and volcanism. Chile also experiences major earthquakes associated with this plate boundary.

### Patagonia

Southern Chile includes fjords, glaciers, temperate forests and the Southern Patagonian Ice Field. Its weather can change rapidly and strong winds are characteristic of many exposed locations.
