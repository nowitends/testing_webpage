## Geography

Chile extends approximately 4,300 km north to south on the South American mainland. Its landscapes include the Atacama Desert, the Andes, the Mediterranean-climate central valley, temperate rainforests, fjords and Patagonian ice fields.

| Zone | Examples | Characteristic |
|---|---|---|
| Far north | Arica, Lauca | High-altitude plateau and desert |
| Norte Grande | Antofagasta, San Pedro de Atacama | Extreme aridity and astronomical observing |
| Norte Chico | La Serena, Elqui Valley | Semi-arid valleys and vineyards |
| Central Chile | Santiago, Valparaíso | Major cities and Mediterranean climate |
| Southern lakes | Valdivia, Puerto Varas | Lakes, volcanoes and temperate forests |
| Patagonia | Aysén, Torres del Paine | Glaciers, mountains and fjords |
| Oceanic territories | Rapa Nui | Polynesian cultural heritage |
