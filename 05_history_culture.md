# History, Culture and Everyday Life

## Selected historical timeline

| Year / period | Event |
|---|---|
| Before European arrival | Diverse Indigenous societies, including Mapuche, Aymara, Rapa Nui and others |
| 1541 | Santiago founded by Pedro de Valdivia |
| 1810 | First Government Junta; beginning of the independence process |
| 1818 | Declaration of independence formally proclaimed |
| 1879–1884 | War of the Pacific |
| 1960 | Valdivia earthquake, magnitude 9.5 (moment magnitude estimate) |
| 1973 | Military coup; beginning of Augusto Pinochet's dictatorship |
| 1988 | Plebiscite rejects continuation of Pinochet's rule |
| 1990 | Return to civilian democratic government |
| 2010 | Major Maule earthquake and tsunami |

## Food and drink

| Name | Category | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Empanada de pino | Savoury pastry | Usually filled with seasoned beef, onions, egg and olives |
| Pastel de choclo | Main dish | Corn-topped baked casserole |
| Cazuela | Soup / stew | Broth with meat, potatoes and vegetables |
| Curanto | Traditional meal | Chiloé preparation combining seafood, meat and potatoes |
| Completo | Street food | Chilean-style hot dog with various toppings |
| Sopaipillas | Snack | Fried dough, often containing pumpkin |
| Mote con huesillos | Sweet drink / dessert | Wheat and dried peaches in sweet liquid |
| Pebre | Condiment | Chopped tomato, onion, herbs and chili |
